# 🌸 ORVEYRA

### **Women’s Health & Safety Intelligence**

> **Understand your health. Own your safety.**

ORVEYRA is an intelligent, privacy-focused platform built to bring **women’s health, personal health understanding, medical information, healthcare discovery, and immediate safety support** into one connected experience.

Instead of functioning as only a period tracker, symptom checker, chatbot, or emergency app, ORVEYRA connects these experiences into a single personal platform.

---

## ✨ The Idea

Women often have pieces of their health information scattered across different places:

* Period tracking apps
* Notes and symptom records
* Laboratory reports
* Doctor visits
* Sleep and stress information
* Health questions
* Emergency situations
* Healthcare discovery

ORVEYRA brings these pieces together.

```text
        TRACK
          ↓
     UNDERSTAND
          ↓
      VISUALIZE
          ↓
        ASK
          ↓
      PREPARE
          ↓
     FIND CARE
          ↓
    GET IMMEDIATE HELP
```

### Our goal

> **Turn scattered health information into an understandable personal health story while keeping the user in control.**

## 🧬 Personal Baseline

Instead of relying only on generic averages, ORVEYRA can build a picture of the user's own historical pattern.

For example:

```text
Typical cycle       29–33 days
Typical sleep        7–8 hours
Typical stress       Low–Moderate
Typical symptoms     Low
```

This allows future changes to be understood relative to the user's own history.

---

# 🌊 Body Drift™

**Body Drift™** highlights meaningful changes from an established personal pattern.

Example:

```text
PERSONAL BASELINE

Cycle        🟢
Sleep        🟢
Stress       🟢
Symptoms     🟢
Biomarkers   🟢

            ↓

CURRENT PATTERN

Cycle        🟠
Sleep        🟡
Stress       🟡
Symptoms     🟠
Biomarkers   🟠
```

The system is designed to communicate:

> **“Your recorded pattern has changed.”**

rather than making unsupported medical diagnoses.

# ⏪ Health Rewind

### **“When did my pattern begin changing?”**

Health Rewind lets the user explore their previous health history and identify the earliest detectable changes in their recorded timeline.

```text
TODAY
Persistent change
      ↓
2 months ago
Symptoms changed
      ↓
4 months ago
Cycle variability increased
      ↓
6 months ago
```

The goal is to help users understand **when something changed**, without pretending to establish medical causation.

---

# 🛡️ Responsible AI

ORVEYRA is not designed to act as an autonomous doctor.

The system should not:

* diagnose a condition from one symptom
* claim that one biomarker proves a disease
* prescribe medication
* tell users to stop medication
* replace professional healthcare
* fabricate medical probabilities
* automatically contact authorities
* automatically share private health information

Instead, ORVEYRA should communicate in terms such as:

> **Observed change**

> **Possible trend**

> **Persistent pattern**

> **Insufficient data**

> **Missing context**

> **Discuss with a healthcare professional**

# 🧠 Why ORVEYRA?

The core problem isn't simply the absence of another tracker.

The deeper problem is that women's health information is often **fragmented**.

A cycle exists in one app.

A blood report is inside a PDF.

Symptoms are remembered differently.

A doctor appointment happens months later.

An urgent situation is recorded somewhere else.

ORVEYRA aims to connect those experiences while keeping the user in control.
